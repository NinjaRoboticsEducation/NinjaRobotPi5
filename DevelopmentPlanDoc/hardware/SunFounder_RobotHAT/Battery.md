## About the Battery

**Battery**

[![../_images/3pin_battery1.jpg](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/robot-hat-v4/en/latest/_images/3pin_battery1.jpg)](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/robot-hat-v4/en/latest/_images/3pin_battery1.jpg)
- **VCC**: Battery positive terminal, here there are two sets of VCC and GND is to increase the current and reduce the resistance.
- **Middle**: To balance the voltage between the two cells and thus protect the battery.
- **GND**: Negative battery terminal.

This is a custom battery pack made by SunFounder consisting of two 18650 batteries with a capacity of 2000mAh. The connector is XH2.54 3 pin, which can be charged directly after being inserted into the shield.
